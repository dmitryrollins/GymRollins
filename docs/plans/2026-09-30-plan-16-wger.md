# Plan 16: wger exercise photos (per-item CC licenses) and multilingual exercise names

Date: 2026-09-30 · Branch `feat-wger` (worktree `wt-wger`) · Wave 2.

## Goal

1. Fill photo gaps: library exercises that have neither an own (AI) photo nor a free-exercise-db photo get a
   photo from wger.de when one confidently shows the same exercise, with the credit each image's license requires.
2. Multilingual names: the Library search and the exercise picker find exercises by their names in other
   languages (German "Bankdrücken" → Barbell Bench Press), and the CSV importers use those names as a last exact
   alias before fuzzy suggestions.

## Data and licenses

- Source: the public wger REST API (`https://wger.de/api/v2/`: `exerciseinfo`, `license`, `version`), **data only**.
  wger's code is AGPL-3.0 and none of it is copied. Snapshot: 2026-09-30, API/server version `2.8.0-dev.0`
  (recorded in both generated JSON files and the review doc).
- Every exercise translation and every image carries its own license and author. Permitted:
  CC-BY-SA 3 (id 1), CC-BY-SA 4 (id 2), CC0 (id 3), CC-BY 4 (id 4). **Not used:** ODbL (id 5, a database license,
  not an image/text license), anything unknown, anything with an empty `license_author` (no one to credit), and
  images wger flags `is_ai_generated` (we only label our own AI photos; mislabeling someone else's would be wrong).
- Obligations (CC-BY/BY-SA): title, author, source link, license link, and whether the work was modified. The app
  loads the original file and scales/crops it to the card → every credit says "modified: resized/cropped for
  display". ShareAlike applies to the images themselves (we don't redistribute copies: they are loaded on demand
  from wger's media server, like free-exercise-db from jsDelivr), and does not reach the app's code.
- Names: per-translation license + author are carried into `exercise-names-i18n.json`; the app shows them on the
  exercise detail screen ("Other names") and Settings → Credits has a dataset-level credit.

### Image provenance (found during the build)

A visual review of every license-clean image the matcher picked showed that many wger uploads are commercial
images re-uploaded under a CC license by someone else (a "© HOIST Fitness Systems" notice, weighttraining.guide
watermarks, StrengthLog branding, Fitness-Programmer-style 3D renders from many uploaders, AI art not flagged as
AI). A license from a non-owner is void, so images also need a trusted author (Everkinetic, wger.de) or an
individual review: `scripts/wger-image-policy.json`. Result: 7 new photos instead of 24. Names are unaffected.

## Build (`scripts/map-wger.mjs`, Node, no dependencies)

- `--fetch`: pages through `exerciseinfo` (limit 200), reads `license` and `version`, HEADs every usable image for
  its byte size, and writes a trimmed snapshot (ids, names/aliases per language with license/author, equipment and
  muscle ids, image metadata) to `media/wger/` (gitignored, like the generated photos); its sha256 is recorded.
- Default: builds from the snapshot →
  - `ios/GymRollins/Resources/wger-photos.json`: `ourId → [{url, title, author, license, licenseUrl, sourceUrl,
    modified}]`, max 2 images per exercise: the main image first, then another image by the same author under the
    same license (so one caption covers both). Originals > 4 MB (8 of 277) use wger's 400 px thumbnail instead
    (the app's photo store rejects > 5 MB).
  - `ios/GymRollins/Resources/exercise-names-i18n.json`: `ourId → {names: {lang: [{n, a, l}]}, url}`
    (English excluded; wger translation aliases included).
  - `docs/import/wger-review.md`: counts, license breakdown, curated decisions, owner routine coverage, unmatched
    sample.
- Matching (conservative, reuses `map-exercise-photos.mjs`'s `nameTokens`/`matchKey`):
  1. Curated alias `scripts/wger-aliases.json` (`ourId → {wger: [ids] | null, why}`); `null` vetoes.
  2. Automatic: same normalized English name key (word order, plurals, synonyms, equipment words handled as in the
     free-exercise-db matcher) **and** equipment agrees (wger equipment list + equipment words in the name;
     only-neutral kit such as bench/mat/pull-up bar = unknown → body weight or machine) **and** muscles agree
     (our target+secondary muscles intersect wger's primary+secondary). Several wger entries with the same key are
     duplicates of one exercise and are merged; a wger entry hit by our exercises with different equipment is
     dropped.
- Tests: `scripts/map-wger.test.mjs` with inline fixtures (license/author/AI filtering, equipment + muscle
  agreement, alias/veto, duplicates, thumbnail fallback, output fields).

## App

- `ExerciseMedia.Source.wger`, `ExerciseMedia.credits: [PhotoCredit]`. `isAIGenerated` stays `source == .own`.
- `WgerPhotoProvider` (bundled `wger-photos.json`; https + host `wger.de` only; malformed entries dropped).
- **Provider order: record → Storage → bundled own → free-exercise-db → wger → illustration.** wger only fills
  gaps: free-exercise-db photos are consistent real start/end photo pairs; wger's are a mix of line drawings
  (Everkinetic), 3D renders and phone photos, often a single frame, so they are not clearly better. They still
  serve as a fallback candidate when a free-exercise-db photo can't load.
- wger frames aren't guaranteed start/end positions → VoiceOver says "Photo 1 of 2" for them. Most are line
  drawings on transparency, so they're shown whole (fit, not fill) on white, also as thumbnails.
- Attribution: `ExercisePhotoView(showsCredit: true)` (exercise detail, which is also the workout's How-To
  screen) overlays a small "Photo: <author> · <license>" capsule; tapping opens a sheet with title, author,
  license (link), source (link) and "Resized/cropped for display". List thumbnails and the logging hero have no
  caption (the hero's How-To button opens the detail with the credit).
- Names: `ExerciseLibrary(exercises:, otherNames:)`; `search` matches the English name or any other-language name
  (diacritic-insensitive: "bankdrucken" works too); `exerciseId(forOtherName:)` = exact normalized, unambiguous.
  `ImportPlan` resolution: app alias table → matcher exact/alias → other-language exact → fuzzy suggestion.
- Displayed names stay English (avoid confusing the owner; no device-language display); other names show only
  on the detail screen's Instructions tab under "Other names", with their credit (authors, licenses, wger link).
- Credits: CREDITS.md gets a wger line (dataset credit; per-photo credit on the photo).
