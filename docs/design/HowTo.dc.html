Barbell Bench Press

1 Lie back on a flat bench with your eyes under the bar and feet flat on the floor.
2 Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width and unrack it over your chest.
3 Lower the bar with control until it lightly touches your mid-chest, keeping elbows near a 45° angle.
4 Press the bar back up to full arm extension, driving through your feet and squeezing your chest.
Target: chest · Secondary: triceps, front delts
